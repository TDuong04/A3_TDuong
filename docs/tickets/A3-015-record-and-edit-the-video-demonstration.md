# A3-015 — Record and edit the video demonstration

## Context

5 points, and the second-largest single row after creativity. Entirely independent of how
good the agents are, provided the required moments are on screen.

All three members must appear and present at least one part, so this needs scheduling, not just
recording.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Under 10 minutes; all three members appear and each presents at least one part
- [ ] Gridworld in a Pygame window with a Q-learning or SARSA agent and correct item/monster behaviour
- [ ] Clear evidence of a learned policy, not random actions — narrate the policy overlay
- [ ] Arena with the trained agent: enemies, projectiles, collisions, and one phase progression
- [ ] Both control schemes demonstrated, two short clips is fine
- [ ] Uploaded unlisted to YouTube or OneDrive and the link placed in the report PDF

## Notes

The models shown must be the ones committed in `models/`.
